Hi Alessio,

results of your latest main on my MacBookAir6,1 (BCM4360, core 42, radio
2069 rev 4, kernel 7.2.10), and my re-cut.

c452fdd
- 5 GHz (ch64, VHT 80 MHz, 2 streams, -78 dBm): download 70 Mbit/s
  (63b6e32: 2.45), upload 58. So A-MPDU RX fixed the download.
- 2.4 GHz (ch11, -50 dBm): the PHY TX fault is gone, no errors, RX works
  (10.4k frames; before: none). DHCP and ping are clean. Throughput is
  poor: upload 3.0-3.4, download 5.1 Mbit/s, rates stay legacy (tx 54,
  rx 24), no HT on 2.4 GHz. My tree has HT capabilities on that band and
  runs HT MCS 14/15 at 54-63 Mbit/s.
- One thing I did not isolate: "band change is a full init" restarted the
  core 15 times during the first scan. Afterwards the station stayed
  associated, but ARP to the router went unanswered for minutes on
  2.4 GHz. A fresh load worked at once.
- Not tested: hardware CCMP, A-MPDU TX, thermal and radar.

Re-cut
I cut my series as you proposed: 14 patches in four series on v7.3-rc5
plus the use-after-free fix, which I sent to linux-wireless yesterday.
 A generic: MAC filter after restart, DMA64 bus-address engines (no
   comments), RX refill retry.
 B core 40/42: DMA burst bits, MAC core setup, address match
   table/templates/beacons.
 C formats: chanspec, TX descriptor/status, RX header, A-MPDU.
 D PHY: radio 2069 and tables, init and 2.4 GHz, 5 GHz/80 MHz/VHT, drop
   BROKEN.
MAC code is gated on core revision 40/42 or ucode >= 784 (only 832
tested), the PHY type only for the PHY. Each patch compiles alone, and the
whole set runs on my board without module parameters: 2.4 GHz HT 63 up /
54 down, 5 GHz VHT-80 40-53 up / 58-70 down, no errors in the log.
Series A follows in a separate mail thread; B to D after we agreed on A.
