Hi Alessio,

I tested the 8 bcma patches on a MacBookAir6,1 (BCM4360 rev 3, 7.2.10),
with the b43 from our series. The tree is v7.3-rc5 bcma plus your
patches, built as an out-of-tree bcma.ko.

- Builds, loads and connects. Cores, PHY and firmware come up cleanly.
  There are no HT or PLL timeouts and no "Microcode not responding".
  Result: 65 Mbit/s MCS 7 on 2.4 GHz, and a 30 MB download at about
  32 Mbit/s.
- Resume: a 3 h 42 min suspend with the lid closed, and the chip came
  back alive after resume. Stock bcma left it dead after a longer
  (11 h) sleep. This is one run, so I'll repeat it overnight.
- Untested: patches 5-7 (our b43 doesn't use the new SPROM fields) and
  patch 8 (my chip is a 4360).
